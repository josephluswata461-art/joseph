Our Admission Process

We aim to make the admission process straightforward for
parents and guardians.

Step 1: Choose Your Preferred Branch

Visit or contact the Beatrice Schools branch where you
would like to enrol your child.

Step 2: Make an Admission Enquiry

Contact the school administration to learn about available classes,
admission requirements,school fees, and reporting dates.

Step 3: Obtain an Admission Form

Collect the admission form from the relevant branch and complete
it with accurate information about the learner.

Step 4:Sudmit the Required Documents

Submit the completed admission form and the documents requested
by the school administration.

Step 5:Receive Admission Guidance

The school administration will review the application and explain
the next steps, including any remaining requirements.
